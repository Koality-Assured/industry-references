# Maine

This capture is a point-in-time locator for attorney review, not legal advice and not a filing.

## Identity

The Revisor of Statutes publishes the Constitution of the State of Maine and the Maine Revised Statutes. The constitution's judicial article vests the judicial power in a Supreme Judicial Court and such other courts as the Legislature establishes. The Judicial Branch opinions page is the Supreme Judicial Court, and it addresses the Clerk of the Law Court. No separate intermediate-appellate opinion site was fetched.

## Constitution

The Revisor's constitution text, arranged by the Chief Justice and approved by Resolve 2023, chapter 86, is a preamble plus articles. The fetched text shows: I Declaration of Rights; II Electors; III Distribution of Powers; IV legislative power in three parts (House, Senate, and legislative power); V executive power; VI Judicial Power; VII Military; VIII education and municipal home rule; IX General Provisions; X Additional Provisions.

## Administrative code

The Secretary of State posts adopted agency rules. The rulemaking page describes the Code of Maine Rules by department, unit, and chapter. Session laws are the Laws of Maine volumes from the Revisor.

## Courts and dockets

The opinions page says online opinions are subject to revision and that the permanent official versions, since 1966, are those in the Atlantic Reporter. It does not state a website fee. Maine eCourts public portal offers Smart Search for traffic cases statewide and for cases in courts where eFiling is implemented. The fetched portal page does not state a search fee. Trial courts are not given a single name on that portal page.

## Court rules and attorney general opinions

Court rules are on the Judicial Branch rules index. Attorney general opinions and memoranda are collected by the Law and Legislative Reference Library.

## Matter map

Criminal matters: Titles 17 and 17-A, and Title 15 for criminal court procedure. Civil matters: Title 14, with evidence in Title 16. Administrative matters: Title 5 and the Secretary of State agency-rule compilation. Municipal and county structure is Title 30-A, which is not a municipal ordinance code.

## Local government

Municipal ordinances are city-specific and must be located from that municipality's official code host. No fetched official page stated a county total. No county ordinance URL is listed.

# New Jersey

This capture is a point-in-time locator for attorney review, not legal advice and not a filing.

## Identity

The Legislature publishes the New Jersey Constitution in articles numbered I through XI. The fetched judicial text vests the judicial power in a Supreme Court, a Superior Court, and other courts of limited jurisdiction, and it divides the Superior Court into an Appellate Division, a Law Division, and a Chancery Division. The courts site says the New Jersey Supreme Court is the state's highest appellate court.

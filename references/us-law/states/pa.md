# Pennsylvania

This capture is a point-in-time locator for attorney review, not legal advice and not a filing.

## Identity

The General Assembly publishes the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes and the Constitution of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. The Supreme Court page says the Supreme Court of Pennsylvania is the highest court in the Commonwealth and the oldest appellate court in the nation. The Unified Judicial System also publishes Superior Court and Commonwealth Court pages.

## Administrative code

The Pennsylvania Code and Bulletin site is the regulatory compilation. The fetched Bulletin description includes agency rulemakings, gubernatorial documents, General Assembly actions, and statewide and local court rules.

## Courts and dockets

Trial courts named on the Judiciary Web Portal: common pleas courts and magisterial district courts. The portal also covers appellate-court docket sheets. It says the portal provides the public with access to those docket sheets and does not state a search fee. It separately names state probation and parole fees. Supreme Court opinions are posted by the UJS; the opinions page says official opinions are available from the Supreme Court prothonotaries.

## Court rules and attorney general opinions

Statewide and local court rules are part of the Pennsylvania Code and Bulletin description. Pennsylvania Code Title 201 is Rules of Judicial Administration and returned HTTP 200. That page says publication in the title gives the text no special status under the Commonwealth Documents Law, and that the official text of judicial documents is elsewhere. `https://www.pacourts.us/courts/supreme-court/court-rules` returned HTTP 404. Official Attorney General opinions are published by the Office of Attorney General.

## Matter map

Criminal matters: Title 18, Crimes and Offenses, and Title 42 for judiciary and judicial procedure. Civil and court structure: Title 42. Administrative matters: Title 2 and the Pennsylvania Code. Counties, cities, boroughs, townships, and municipalities generally are Titles 16, 11, 8, 73, and 53. Those titles are state statutes, not municipal ordinances.

## Local government

Municipal ordinances are city-specific and must be located from that municipality's official code host. No fetched official page stated a county total. No county ordinance URL is listed.

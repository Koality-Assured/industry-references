# Washington law

Advisory only. This is a point-in-time map of official entry points captured on 2026-10-07. It is not legal advice and it does not reproduce statute text.

## Identity

The Legislature's RCW page describes the Revised Code of Washington as the compilation of permanent laws now in force and says the Statute Law Committee treats the certified PDF archive as the official publication. The same office posts the Washington Administrative Code and session laws. The courts post the Supreme Court, the Court of Appeals divisions, slip opinions, and an Odyssey case-search portal.

## Administrative code

The [Washington Administrative Code](https://apps.leg.wa.gov/wac/default.aspx) returned GET 200. The page says the Statute Law Committee treats the certified PDF archive as the official WAC publication and that the online WAC is updated twice a month.

## Courts and dockets

The court of last resort is the [Washington Supreme Court](https://www.courts.wa.gov/appellate_trial_courts/SupremeCourt/). Intermediate appellate courts are the Court of Appeals, Divisions I, II, and III, listed on [Appellate and trial courts](https://www.courts.wa.gov/appellate_trial_courts/). [Slip opinions](https://www.courts.wa.gov/opinions/) are a separate site from the [Odyssey Portal](https://odysseyportal.courts.wa.gov/odyportal) case search. The opinions page says slip opinions are not necessarily the final opinion and that the Washington Reports and Washington Appellate Reports replace them. The portal root without `/odyportal` returned an IIS placeholder and is not the case-search application.

## Matter map

- Courts: Title 2, Courts of Record; Title 3, District Courts—Courts of Limited Jurisdiction.
- Administrative: Title 34, Administrative Law, and the Washington Administrative Code.

## Local government

RCW Title 35, Cities and Towns, and Title 35A, Optional Municipal Code, are state statutes. This capture does not list city or county ordinance-code URLs. Obtain ordinances from the city or county clerk.

# District of Columbia

Advisory capture of official entry points. This is not legal advice and it does not reproduce statutory, regulatory, or opinion text.

## Identity

The District of Columbia is a federal district, not a state. The Council of the District of Columbia publishes the Code of the District of Columbia through the D.C. Law Library. The code page states that it is current through September 26, 2026, with last codified D.C. Law 26-187. Title 1, Chapter 2 is the Home Rule chapter. The District of Columbia Courts publish the Court of Appeals, which is the District's highest court and is not the Supreme Court of the United States.

## Constitution

The District of Columbia has no state constitution. The organic structure captured here is the Home Rule chapter, Title 1, Chapter 2 of the Code of the District of Columbia, titled "District of Columbia Home Rule."

## Administrative code

The Office of Documents and Administrative Issuances publishes the District of Columbia Municipal Regulations and the District of Columbia Register at DCRegs.

## Courts and dockets

The District of Columbia Court of Appeals is the court of last resort for District law. It is not the Supreme Court of the United States. There is no intermediate appellate court between the Superior Court and the Court of Appeals. The Courts site publishes Court of Appeals opinions and memoranda of judgments, a Superior Court case search, and a Court of Appeals case search. A path `/us/dc/council/laws` on the code host returned HTTP 404; Council legislation is cataloged from the Council site instead.

## Rules and attorney general opinions

Rules of the DC Court of Appeals are on the Courts site. Formal opinions of the Attorney General are on the Office of the Attorney General site.

## Local government

The District is a federal district and has no counties. The Council of the District of Columbia is the local legislature. This capture does not supply advisory-neighborhood or agency ordinance URLs beyond the Code, the Council legislation page, and DCRegs.

# Iowa

This is a point-in-time locator captured on 2026-10-07. It is not legal advice and it is not a filing.

The Iowa Legislature publishes the Constitution of the State of Iowa, the Iowa Code, Iowa Acts, administrative rules, and court rules. The 2026 Iowa Code page separates an official PDF version from unofficial PDF and RTF versions. The Iowa Judicial Branch publishes the Supreme Court and the Court of Appeals. Iowa Courts Online Search is the electronic docket search.

## Rules and attorney general opinions

Court rules are listed by the legislature at `/law/courtRules/courtRulesListings`. The Attorney General opinions page describes formal opinions as answers to legal questions of a public nature about a public official's duties. The legislature also publishes an opinions archive at `https://www.legis.iowa.gov/publications/attorneyGeneralOpinions` (HTTP 200).

## Matter map

Criminal law and procedure are Title XVI. The judicial branch and judicial procedures are Title XV. Court rules supply procedure and evidence practice on the court-rules list. Administrative rules are the Iowa Administrative Rules.

## Local government

Title IX is Local Government, chapters 331 through 420. Those chapters are state law about local units. Ordinances are adopted by the unit. This capture does not supply a municipal code URL.

# Oregon law

Advisory only. This is a point-in-time map of official entry points captured on 2026-10-07. It is not legal advice and it does not reproduce statute text.

## Identity

The Legislative Assembly posts the Oregon Constitution, the Oregon Revised Statutes, and Oregon Laws (session laws). The ORS page says the printed ORS is the official text and that the database is not the official text. The Secretary of State posts Oregon Administrative Rules. The Judicial Department posts the Supreme Court, the Court of Appeals, and separate opinion pages.

## Courts and dockets

The court of last resort is the [Oregon Supreme Court](https://www.courts.oregon.gov/courts/appellate/supreme/Pages/default.aspx). The intermediate appellate court is the [Oregon Court of Appeals](https://www.courts.oregon.gov/courts/appellate/coa/Pages/default.aspx). Opinions are posted separately: [Supreme Court opinions](https://www.courts.oregon.gov/publications/sc/Pages/default.aspx) and [Court of Appeals opinions](https://www.courts.oregon.gov/publications/coa/Pages/default.aspx). Those opinion pages are separate from [OJCIN OnLine](https://www.courts.oregon.gov/services/online/Pages/ojcin.aspx). This capture did not confirm a subscription fee from the OJCIN page.

## Matter map

- Criminal matters: ORS Title 14, Procedure in Criminal Matters Generally, and Title 16, Crimes and Punishments.
- Civil matters: Title 2, Procedure in Civil Proceedings, and Title 4, Evidence and Witnesses.
- Courts: Title 1, Courts of Record; Court Officers; Juries.
- Administrative matters: Oregon Administrative Rules. The ORS title table does not use a single title caption limited to administrative procedure.

## Local government

ORS Title 20, Counties and County Officers, and Title 21, Cities, are state law. This capture does not list city or county ordinance-code URLs. Obtain ordinances from the city recorder or county clerk.

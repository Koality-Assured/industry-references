# Delaware

Advisory capture of official entry points. This is not legal advice and it does not reproduce statutory, regulatory, or opinion text.

## Identity

The Delaware Code Online is published by the Delaware Legislature. The code home page states that authenticated PDFs of the Delaware Code, the Laws of Delaware, the Constitution of Delaware, and the Delaware Administrative Code are available under the Uniform Electronic Legal Material Act, effective October 21, 2014.

## Administrative code

The Delaware Administrative Code is published at regulations.delaware.gov. The Code home page states that authenticated PDFs cover the Administrative Code as well as the Code, the Laws of Delaware, and the Constitution.

## Courts and dockets

The Delaware Supreme Court is the court of last resort. The Delaware Courts site lists the Supreme Court, Court of Chancery, Superior Court, Family Court, Court of Common Pleas, and Justice of the Peace Court. It does not list an intermediate appellate court. Opinions and orders are on the courts opinions index. Civil case search is described on the docket page as CourtConnect access for the Superior Court, Court of Common Pleas, and Justice of the Peace Court.

## Rules and attorney general opinions

Court rules are published by the Delaware Courts. Attorney general opinions are published by the Delaware Department of Justice.

## Matter map

- Criminal: Title 11 - Crimes and Criminal Procedure.
- Civil: Title 10 - Courts and Judicial Procedure.
- Administrative: Delaware Administrative Code, and Title 29 - State Government.

## Local government

Title 9 covers counties and Title 22 covers municipalities as state law. Municipal charters are published on the state charters site. County and municipal ordinances are adopted by those governments. This capture does not supply ordinance-host URLs.

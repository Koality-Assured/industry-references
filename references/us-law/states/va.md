# Virginia

Advisory capture of official entry points. This is not legal advice and it does not reproduce statutory, regulatory, or opinion text.

## Identity

The Virginia General Assembly Legislative Information System publishes the Code of Virginia, the Constitution of Virginia, and the Virginia Administrative Code. The Virginia Court System publishes the Rules of the Supreme Court of Virginia and a Court of Appeals of Virginia page.

## Administrative code

The Virginia Administrative Code is published on the same Legislative Information System. The fetched page says the General Assembly provides it online and that it is updated daily.

## Courts and dockets

The Rules page identifies the Supreme Court of Virginia as the rulemaking court. The Court of Appeals of Virginia page returned HTTP 200 and is the intermediate appellate court. The opinions path `https://www.vacourts.gov/opinions/home`, which the court front page links, returned HTTP 403, so no opinions URL is cataloged. The OCIS landing URL returned HTTP 200 with a short untitled body. `https://eapps.courts.state.va.us/cav-public` returned HTTP 403.

## Rules and attorney general opinions

Rules of the Supreme Court of Virginia are on the Virginia Court System site. Official opinions of the Attorney General are on the Attorney General's site.

## Matter map

- Criminal: Title 18.2 - Crimes and Offenses Generally. Title 19.2 - Criminal Procedure.
- Civil: Title 8.01 - Civil Remedies and Procedure.
- Courts: Title 16.1 - Courts Not of Record. Title 17.1 - Courts of Record.
- Administrative: Virginia Administrative Code, and Title 2.2 - Administration of Government.
- The code index has no title captioned Evidence.

## Local government

Title 15.2 covers counties, cities, and towns as state law. City and town charters are on the Legislative Information System charters page. Local ordinances are adopted by the locality. This capture does not supply county ordinance-host URLs.

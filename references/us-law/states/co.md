# Colorado law sources

This page is an advisory point-in-time map of official sources. It is not legal advice and it does not reproduce statutory text.

## Identity

Colorado (CO) is a state. The court of last resort is the Colorado Supreme Court. The Judicial Branch publishes a Court of Appeals. Trial courts and water courts are separate entries on the judicial site.

## Administrative code

The Secretary of State Code of Colorado Regulations welcome page is the official administrative-code publication. The same host has a numerical department list and the Colorado Register. The state rulemaking portal explains the Administrative Procedure Act process and links back to the CCR.

## Courts and dockets

The Supreme Court and Court of Appeals have their own judicial-branch pages. Docket search is a judicial-branch page. Supreme Court opinions are published on the judicial site, which also points to a case-law search.

## Rules and attorney general

Adopted and proposed rule changes are on the Supreme Court rules page. Attorney general opinions are on the Department of Law site. The rulemaking portal also says the Colorado Register publishes Attorney General rules opinions.

## Matter map

The criminal code is Title 18 and criminal proceedings are Title 16. Courts and court procedure, including the civil and evidence material gathered under that title, are Title 13. Administrative rules are the CCR. Title 24 is Government - State.

## Local government

Title 29 is Government - Local, Title 30 is counties, and Title 31 is municipalities. To locate ordinances for a named city, use that city's official website and the city clerk, or the municipal code the city itself publishes. This capture does not record county or city code URLs.

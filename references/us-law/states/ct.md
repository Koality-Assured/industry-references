# Connecticut

This capture is a point-in-time locator for attorney review, not legal advice and not a filing.

## Identity

The General Assembly publishes the Constitution of the State of Connecticut and the General Statutes of Connecticut. The Judicial Branch home page lists the Supreme Court, the Appellate Court, and the Superior Court. The fetched Supreme Court chapter describes that court as having final and conclusive jurisdiction of matters brought before it according to law.

## Administrative code

The eRegulations portal browses the Regulations of Connecticut State Agencies. The Uniform Administrative Procedure Act page that was fetched points agencies to posting on that system.

## Courts and dockets

Trial court named on the Judicial Branch home page: Superior Court. Intermediate court: Appellate Court. Court of last resort: Supreme Court. Opinions of both appellate courts are posted as advance-release opinions by the Judicial Branch law library. Civil, family, housing, and small-claims look-up is one site. Criminal and motor-vehicle look-up, including pending cases and daily dockets, is another. Neither fetched page states a search fee.

## Court rules and attorney general opinions

Court rules are the Connecticut Practice Book. The Attorney General publishes formal opinions and states that General Statutes section 3-125 authorizes opinions to legislative leaders and to heads of state agencies, boards, and commissions.

## Matter map

Criminal matters: Titles 53, 53a, and 54, plus appellate opinions. Civil matters: Title 52 and the civil look-up. Administrative matters: the eRegulations compilation and Title 4 agency-management statutes. Family and probate matters are Titles 46b and 45a, not the criminal titles.

## Local government

Municipal ordinances are city-specific and must be located from that municipality's official code host. Title 7 is the state municipal-law title, not a city code. The Secretary of the State historical-counties URL returned HTTP 200, and the fetched body did not state a county total. No county ordinance URL is listed.

# Kansas law sources

This page is an advisory point-in-time map of official sources. It is not legal advice and it does not reproduce statutory text.

## Identity

Kansas (KS) is a state. The court of last resort is the Kansas Supreme Court, which the Revisor's constitution page publishes as an article of the constitution. An intermediate Court of Appeals exists in that constitutional structure. This capture could not open the Judicial Branch website.

## Administrative code

The Secretary of State FAQ says current temporary and permanent Kansas Administrative Regulations are on the online KAR page and links `https://rules.ks.gov/`. That URL returned HTTP 200 as a short shell. Older KAR paths on `kssos.org` and `sos.ks.gov` returned HTTP 405 and are not used.

## Courts and dockets

`https://kscourts.gov/`, `https://www.kscourts.gov/`, and the appellate public-access path returned HTTP 403. `https://www.kscourts.org/` failed before an HTTP status because the TLS channel could not be created. No court, docket, or court-rules URL is cataloged. The constitution page remains the verified official text for the judicial article.

## Rules and attorney general

Court rules were not verified. `https://www.ag.ks.gov/reports-resources/ag-opinions` returned HTTP 403, so no attorney general opinions URL is cataloged.

## Matter map

Criminal offenses are Chapter 21, Crimes and Punishments, and criminal procedure is Chapter 22, Criminal Procedure. The index also still lists Chapter 62, Procedure, Criminal. Civil procedure is Chapter 60, Procedure, Civil. Courts are Chapter 20, Courts. Administrative procedure is Chapter 77, Statutes; Administrative Rules and Regulations and Procedure. The regulations themselves are on the KAR portal.

## Local government

Chapters 12 through 15 cover cities, and Chapter 18 covers counties. To locate ordinances for a named city, use that city's official website and the city clerk, or the municipal code the city itself publishes. This capture does not record county or city code URLs.

# Illinois

This is a point-in-time locator captured on 2026-10-07. It is not legal advice and it is not a filing.

The General Assembly publishes the compiled-statutes database, public acts, and the constitution pages. The statutes page says the database was created for members and staff, has not been edited for publication, and is not the official text of the Illinois Compiled Statutes as enacted. The Supreme Court of Illinois is the court of last resort. The Appellate Court is the intermediate appellate court.

## Constitution

The constitution page is titled "Constitution of the State of Illinois" and says it was adopted at a special election on December 15, 1970. It links a preamble and Articles I through XIV: Bill of Rights, The Powers of the State, Suffrage and Elections, The Legislature, The Executive, The Judiciary, Local Government, Finance, Revenue, Education, Environment, Militia, General Provisions, and Constitutional Revision.

## Courts and dockets

The Supreme Court of Illinois is the court of last resort. The Appellate Court is the intermediate appellate court. The docket page is titled "Supreme Court Docket & Briefs."

## Rules and attorney general opinions

Supreme Court Rules are published by the Office of the Illinois Courts. The Attorney General site publishes Official Opinions and says the page covers opinions from 1971 to the present.

## Matter map

Criminal offenses are Chapter 720. Criminal procedure is Chapter 725. Civil procedure is Chapter 735. Courts are Chapter 705. The statute index has no separate evidence chapter. Administrative rules are the Illinois Administrative Code.

## Local government

Chapter 50 is Local Government, Chapter 55 is Counties, Chapter 60 is Townships, and Chapter 65 is Municipalities. Chapter 65 is state law about municipalities. City and county ordinances are adopted locally. This capture does not supply a municipal code URL.

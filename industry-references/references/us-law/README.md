# US Law References

The United States primary-law reference corpus has been federated to its canonical domain spoke repository:

## Federated Scope

The primary-law reference library, including all 50 state court and statutory locators, District of Columbia, federal statutory frameworks (Constitution, USC, CFR, Federal Register, SCOTUS, inferior courts), source ranking rules, and matter classification schemas now live natively within [`legal-router`](https://github.com/Koality-Assured/legal-router).

Legal analysis, contract review, citation verification, redline diffing, and statutory crosswalks are maintained and executed within that spoke under strict licensed attorney supervision and ABA Formal Opinion 512 ethics rules.
